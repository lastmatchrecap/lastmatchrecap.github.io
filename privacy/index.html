Privacy Policy

Last Match Recap · Last updated 10 November 2026

Last Match Recap ("LMR", "we", "us") is a free app that shows highlights of past meetings between your football club and its next opponent. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We keep it short because we collect very little.

Who is responsible

LMR is run by Jonathan, who is the data controller under UK data protection law (UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018). Contact: jonlidesigner@gmail.com.

You can browse feeds without an account. We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or payment details, and we do not sell your data or use it for advertising profiles.

Highlight videos (YouTube)

Highlights play in an embedded YouTube player using YouTube API Services. When a video plays, YouTube (Google) may collect information and set cookies under its own policies. See the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.

Ticket and merchandise links

Some cards link to partner shops for match tickets and club merchandise. If you tap one, you leave LMR and the partner's own privacy policy applies. Partners may use cookies to record that you came from LMR, which is how we earn a commission. We do not receive your purchase or payment details.

Where your data is stored

Account data is stored with our database provider, Supabase, on servers in the EU (Ireland). Our providers act on our instructions and are bound by data protection terms.

Our legal bases

  • Providing the service you asked for: your account, favourite clubs and reminders.
  • Consent for notifications, which you can switch off at any time in your phone's settings.
  • Legitimate interests for technical logs that keep the app secure and working.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data until you delete your account. Technical logs are kept for a short period and then deleted automatically. When you delete your account, we remove your personal data within 30 days.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete or export your data, or object to how we use it. Delete your account in the app, or email jonlidesigner@gmail.com and we will respond within one month. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

Children

LMR is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
